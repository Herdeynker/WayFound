- No client module reads service-role or server-only environment variables.
- No credentials are present in `.env.example`, fixtures or source.
- No `TODO`, `FIXME`, skipped tests or `.only` tests were added for Phase 0.
- The copied canonical documents and three approved images remain available under `docs/wayfound/`.
